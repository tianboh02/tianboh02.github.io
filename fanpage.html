About The Rock

Can You Smell What The Rock Is Cooking?

Dwayne Douglas Johnson, better known as "The Rock", is an American actor, professional wrestler, businessman and former football player.

He first became famous through WWE, where his charisma, wrestling ability and memorable catchphrases helped make him one of professional wrestling's biggest stars.

Johnson later made a successful transition into Hollywood, appearing in major franchises such as Fast & Furious and Jumanji.

Today, he is recognised as an entertainer, entrepreneur, producer and philanthropist.

Career Journey

1996 — WWE Debut

Johnson made his WWE debut as Rocky Maivia.

1997–1999 — The Rock

Rocky Maivia evolved into "The Rock", becoming one of wrestling's biggest personalities.

2001 — Hollywood Breakthrough

Johnson appeared as the Scorpion King in The Mummy Returns.

2010s — Global Movie Star

Roles in Fast & Furious and Jumanji helped establish Johnson as a major Hollywood star.

Today — Business & Entertainment

Johnson continues working across entertainment, business, sports and philanthropy.

The People's Champion
